# IQBasket Family-First Monetization V1

## Product thesis

Families do not buy statistics. They pay to understand a player's development
and to know what to do next. The core value ladder is:

`What happened -> How is the player evolving -> What does it mean -> What next`

IQBasket should therefore acquire through useful free history/statistics, convert
through longitudinal interpretation, and retain through recurring development
plans and visible progress.

## Primary customer

Primary B2C buyer: parent/guardian household.
Permanent product subject: player.
Secondary B2B buyers: team, club and academy.
B2B also acts as a structured data-generation and distribution channel.

The player's longitudinal identity must survive team and club changes.
## Initial product ladder

### Family Free

Purpose: acquisition, trust and habit.
One player, player profile, game history, basic statistics and basic timeline.
The free experience must be genuinely useful; it should not feel like a demo.

### Family

Purpose: convert data into development understanding.
Adds advanced trends, Player 360, goals, development plan, technification,
family-oriented insights and professional report export.

### Family Pro

Purpose: recurring intelligence and action.
Adds AI interpretation, weekly action planning and selected wellness capabilities.
AI is sold as outcomes (insight/weekly plan), never as tokens.
Sensitive nutrition/recovery remains separately governed and is not unlocked by
payment alone.
## B2B ladder

Team products monetize capture, roster, live game, training and staff analytics.
Club/Academy products monetize scale: multi-team administration, governance,
audit, integrations, branding and organizational AI usage.

A B2B plan should not automatically grant Family features to parents. A future
club-funded Family bundle can do that explicitly through `ALL_AUTHORIZED` scope.

## Pricing experiments - not product constants

First willingness-to-pay tests should explore approximately:

- Family: 6.99-8.99 EUR/month.
- Family Pro: 12.99-16.99 EUR/month.
- Annual Family Pro: roughly 2 months free versus monthly.
- Team: 15-30 EUR/month/team depending on analytics level.
- Club: 79-149 EUR/month before enterprise/academy customization.

These are experiment ranges, not values to embed in application code.
## Conversion moments

The strongest upgrade moments should be contextual, not generic paywalls:

1. After enough games exist to show a meaningful trend.
2. When a metric improves or stagnates materially.
3. After a match, when the family wants interpretation rather than raw stats.
4. When a development objective can be quantified and tracked.
5. Before the next training week, when an actionable plan has immediate value.

Examples of value framing:

- "This is how the player has evolved over the last 8 games."
- "This area is improving; this one has stalled."
- "Three priorities for the next training week."

Do not use anxiety, rankings or fear-based messaging to force conversion.
## Metrics for the first monetization pilot

Measure before optimizing price:

- activated households: verified family-player relation plus 3+ viewed sessions;
- data readiness: players with enough game/training evidence for longitudinal value;
- free-to-paid conversion by conversion moment;
- Family versus Family Pro mix;
- 30/90-day paid retention;
- weekly return rate around game and training cycles;
- AI insight generation rate, cost per insight and repeat usage;
- report/plan completion and revisit rate;
- club-originated versus direct-family acquisition;
- support/privacy incidents per 100 active families.

## Recommended first paid experiment

Do not start with a global paywall. Pilot one high-value family surface:
Player 360 longitudinal interpretation plus a weekly development plan, while
keeping basic history and statistics free. This tests willingness to pay for
understanding/action rather than for access to raw data.
