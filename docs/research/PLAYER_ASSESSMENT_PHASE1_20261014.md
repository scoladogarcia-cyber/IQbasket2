# IQBasket – Phase 1: evidence map and player assessment catalogue (working draft)

Date: 2026-10-14. Status: research started; **not** a validated evaluation instrument.

## Verified primary frameworks

1. USA Basketball, *Player Development Curriculum* / *Coaching Guide for All Levels* (2025): Introductory, Foundational, Advanced and Performance stages, with eight learning categories: Ball Handling & Dribbling; Footwork & Body Control; Passing & Receiving; Rebounding; Screening; Shooting; Team Defensive Concepts; Team Offensive Concepts.
   https://www.usab.com/news/2025/10/usa-basketball-coaching-guidebook-player-development-curriculum
2. FIBA / WABC Coaches Manual (international coaching curriculum, available in Spanish; mini basketball and coach levels).
   https://about.fiba.basketball/es/wabc-documents
3. USA Basketball / NBA Youth Guidelines: age-appropriate workload, health and development context; do not turn training-volume metrics into quality scores.
   https://www.usab.com/play/youth-basketball-guidelines

## IQBasket catalogue V0 — provisional, not externally validated

- Ball handling: left/right control, live-dribble speed, protection, change of direction, pressure response.
- Footwork: stops, pivots, balance, movement efficiency.
- Passing: both hands, accuracy, timing, decisions, receiving.
- Shooting: form, free throws, catch-and-shoot, pull-up, shot selection.
- Finishing: dominant/non-dominant hand, contact, layup variants, reads.
- Off-ball offense: spacing, cutting, screening.
- Defense: on-ball stance, containment, help, rotations, closeouts.
- Rebounding: box-out, timing, offensive/defensive rebounding.
- Decision-making: advantage recognition, transitions, read-and-react.
- Physical qualities: speed, coordination, jump, agility; distinguish measured tests from coach observations.
- Behavioral observations: communication, response to feedback, cooperative actions; avoid inferring mental traits from limited observations.

## Rubric requirements before release

Each attribute needs: unambiguous definition; observable behavior; context and opposition; five anchored levels; 'not evaluated' separate from level 1; assessor role and identity; timestamp; source (match/drill/video/test); evidence confidence; age/developmental context; rubric version; and override/audit policy.

For inter-rater reliability, pilot on standardized film clips and drills with independent coaches. Predefine agreement metrics (e.g., weighted kappa for ordinal pair scores, ICC only where appropriate), sample and thresholds. Never claim universal comparability or clinical validity without evidence. Avoid a single universal player score.

## Next research steps

- Map every WABC/USA Basketball skill to a concrete observable attribute.
- Review peer-reviewed talent-identification and reliability studies; record populations, sample sizes, limitations and reproducibility.
- Write drill protocols (attempts, court dimensions, defensive pressure and scoring rules).
- Calibrate coaches with shared video examples; revise ambiguous anchors.
- Separate player self-ratings from coach/scout assessments and longitudinal progress.
